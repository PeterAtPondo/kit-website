Run Kit on your Mac.

Kit is a local, memory-backed collaborator for your coding and chat. It runs entirely on your machine, reads your history, and wakes up already knowing the thread. Private, and yours.

Download for macOS Read the notes

version 0.2.28 · universal (Apple Silicon and Intel)

What you are installing

A small macOS app that sets up a private Kit on your machine: a local database, an API, and an MCP server, all running in Docker. Once it is up, any agent that speaks MCP (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code) can plug into the same memory. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you send it.

Before you start

macOS 13 or later

Ventura or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. The app is a universal build, so the same download runs on both.

Docker Desktop

Kit runs locally in containers, so it needs Docker. If you do not have it, Kit offers to install it for you during setup, or you can get it first from docker.com.

A model

An Anthropic API key gives the smoothest experience. You can also point Kit at a local model with Ollama. You choose during setup.

Space and a little patience

A few GB of free disk for the local database and models. The first launch builds your private Kit and can take 10 to 15 minutes. After that it is quick.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the disk image above and open it.
  2. Drag Kit into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Kit from Applications. It walks you through the rest: name your Kit, set a password, add your model key, and choose what to bring in. Then it reads your history and wakes.

It is an early build

Kit is in beta, so things will still be a little rough. If something breaks or feels off, I genuinely want to hear it. Email kit@kit-project.com, or use the form on the home page. It goes straight to Peter.
