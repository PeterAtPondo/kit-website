Run Kit on your Mac.

Kit is a local, memory-backed collaborator for your coding and chat. It runs entirely on your machine, reads your history, and wakes up already knowing the thread. Private, and yours.

Download for macOS Read the notes

version 0.2.51 · universal (Apple Silicon and Intel)

What you are installing

A small macOS app that sets up a private Kit on your machine: a local database, an API, and an MCP server, all running in Docker. Once it is up, any agent that speaks MCP (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code) can plug into the same memory. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you send it.

Before you start

macOS 13 or later

Ventura or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. The app is a universal build, so the same download runs on both.

Docker Desktop

Kit runs locally in containers, so it needs Docker. If you do not have it, Kit offers to install it for you during setup, or you can get it first from docker.com.

A model

An Anthropic API key gives the smoothest experience. You can also point Kit at a local model with Ollama. You choose during setup.

Space and a little patience

A few GB of free disk for the local database and models. The first launch builds your private Kit and can take 10 to 15 minutes. After that it is quick.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the disk image above and open it.
  2. Drag Kit into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Kit from Applications. It walks you through the rest: name your Kit, set a password, add your model key, and choose what to bring in. Then it reads your history and wakes.

Release notes

A short, high-level log of what each release brought. Newest first.

0.2.51 June 2026

Bug fixes

  • Restored the hover tooltip in the memory view that the previous release had accidentally hidden.
0.2.50 June 2026

Improvements

  • Kit now captures your coding sessions the moment they happen on more surfaces (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and Qwen), so your timeline stays current instead of waiting for the nightly catch-up.
  • Rename your Kit, and rename or delete knowledge areas, from settings.
  • A styling pass on the menu-bar app: tidier fields, buttons and dropdowns, round timeline markers, and save confirmations you can actually see.
  • Updating now reads "Updating Kit" rather than "Installing".

Bug fixes

  • Tidied history that showed redaction markers or raw data carried in from imported sessions.
  • Renaming your Kit in settings now takes effect.
  • Hover tooltips in the memory view no longer linger when you change tabs.
  • Removed a stray "import more history" button; the repair option now shows only for connected surfaces.
0.2.48 June 2026

Improvements

  • You can now check for updates any time from the menu bar.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed the model picker in settings not showing its options in the menu-bar app.
  • The live facets panel no longer shows entries that are not part of your own Kit.
0.2.47 June 2026

Improvements

  • Kit wakes much sooner during setup: it reads your most recent history first and starts being useful while the rest finishes quietly in the background.
  • A new dreaming view shows when Kit is consolidating, with a calm animation, what it is working on, and a tap to peek at a memory or connection it just made.
  • A quieter first run: no file-permission pop-ups during setup.
  • You can point Kit at your own model: any self-hosted, OpenAI-compatible server (for example a Qwen box on your network).
  • If the model you choose is too slow, Kit now tells you while you pick it, and lets you switch to a faster one without restarting setup.
  • Kit writes in British English and refers to itself by the name you gave it.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a long-standing failure in Kit's nightly dream that could stop it consolidating.
  • Uninstalling Kit no longer crashes, and leaves nothing behind.
  • The health screen now reads correctly while Kit is doing its first consolidation.
  • Setup progress is steadier, and common snags like Docker not running now offer a clear retry instead of a dead end.
  • Faster, more reliable indexing of your conversations.
0.2.29 June 2026

Improvements

  • Health at a glance: a calm status light in the app and the menu bar shows how Kit's dreams, surfaces, and data sources are doing, with one-tap fixes when something needs attention.
  • Surfaces you do not have installed now link you to where to get them, instead of showing a switch that cannot do anything.

Bug fixes

  • You can no longer switch on a coding tool that is not installed on your Mac.
0.2.28 June 2026

Improvements

  • Clearer memory cards on the dashboard.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed a case where new memories could silently fail to save on some models.
  • Fixed your name showing incorrectly on a fresh install.
