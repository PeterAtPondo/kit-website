# Typography

Three families carry every word Kit shows. Fraunces speaks: display, headings, the wordmark and Kit's own lines. Manrope works: body, controls, labels, captions. JetBrains Mono holds still: ids, codes, keys and numbers that must line up. Everything in this section is specification for the component library; no file changes here. "Today" cites the worktree at c46415a and the audit in `scratchpad/audit/` (AUDIT.md, and colour-type-tokens.md for the type findings). Paths are relative to the worktree; `index.html` means `api/ui/index.html`, `main.js` means `api/ui/src/main.js`, `Resources/` means `deploy/personal/macos/Resources/`.

The headline pattern in use is `font-serif text-4xl font-medium text-slate-50 tracking-tight` on ten tabs, `font-serif text-2xl font-medium ... tracking-tight` on compact views and Sources, and `font-serif text-xl font-medium ... tracking-tight` on card titles (over 55 `font-serif` elements the companions are `font-medium` 23 times and `tracking-tight` 20 times). So the serif steps are 36, 24 and 20 at weight 500 with Tailwind's `tracking-tight`, and the sans steps are 14, 12, 11 and 10.5 with half-pixel steps in between. The guide keeps the serif steps, folds the half-pixel sans steps into four, and sets 10.5 px as the floor.

Tailwind 3.4.4 (`api/ui/package.json:19`) defaults used below: `text-xs` 12/16, `text-sm` 14/20, `text-base` 16/24, `text-lg` 18/28, `text-xl` 20/28, `text-2xl` 24/32, `text-4xl` 36/40; `tracking-tight` is -0.025em; `leading-relaxed` 1.625, `leading-snug` 1.375, `leading-none` 1. 1 rem is 16 px. "opsz" is Fraunces' optical size; "auto" means the browser default `font-optical-sizing: auto`, which sets it to the rendered pixel size.
