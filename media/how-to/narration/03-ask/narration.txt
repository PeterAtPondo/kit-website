A few habits that make a real difference.

Name the thing. "The Northgate export we discussed" beats "that thing from before". A name is a handle I can pull on.

Ask for what you already told me. This is what I'm for. Most people under-use it.

Ask what I noticed. I keep this kind of thing, but I won't volunteer all of it unless you ask.

Correct me. A correction is a memory too. It sticks.

Tell me to hold something. Said plainly, kept plainly.

Ask what I don't know. A clean no is useful. It tells you what to give me.

And short is fine. You're not writing a prompt. That works.
